# Studio reference workflow rebuild

## Reference structure to preserve

The supplied Studio concept is an **Editorial Control Room** rather than a conventional dashboard: a warm-paper field and quiet operational rail frame three deliberate zones. The left zone gathers source and direction, the center zone is a charcoal artboard, and the right zone finishes the work through Brand, captions, validation, delivery, and export. The headline is a single restrained editorial statement, not a collection of feature labels.

## Non-negotiable workflow preservation

The redesign must keep the current product search; compose/import switch; format; atmosphere; template; advanced crop, alignment, signal, message, badge, and proof controls; local rendering; imported-visual workflow; neutral/configured Brand behavior; four independent FR/EN/AR/Darija caption editors with saving and copy actions; approval; ZIP export; and delivery progression.

## Design correction

The current control room has the right three-pane ingredients but makes all controls visually equal. The rebuilt page will preserve the complete toolset while using progressive disclosure: source and primary direction stay visible, advanced craft controls sit in a defined "fine tune" shelf, the artboard becomes the visual anchor, and the finishing rail becomes a paced hand-off sequence rather than a long caption stack. No feature is removed; density is managed through grouping and clear action priority.

## Chosen page architecture

| Zone | Reference role | Interaction decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Direction desk** | A compact source-and-brief rail on the left. | Keep source mode, product, format/template, atmosphere, and structure visible. Group advanced crop, type alignment, signal color, message, badge, and proof controls in named `Fine tune` shelves. |
| **Artboard** | The dominant center surface. | Keep the live local SVG preview, rulers, stage state, and five-step progress thread. The primary `Render post` action remains the only lime commitment in this zone. |
| **Finishing desk** | A pale, editorial hand-off rail at the right—matching the supplied reference rather than repeating a second dark inspector. | Brand stays at the top. Caption generation becomes an explicit second hand-off; independent language editors are organized with language tabs so only one detailed editor is open at a time. Validation, Social hand-off, and export remain visible as the final block. |

The language editor tabs are a workflow enhancement, not a removal: all four records remain independent, retained in state, saved on blur, and copyable. Tabs reduce false visual density and make the reference’s finishing rail readable at desktop width and actionable on narrow screens.
